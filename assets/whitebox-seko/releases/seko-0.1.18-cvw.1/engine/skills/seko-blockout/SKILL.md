# Seko scene previs

Use the release's actual documented engine interface. Author with [the source contract](references/source-contract.md), inspect with [review requirements](references/review.md), and refine with [local revisions](references/revisions.md). The full release CLI is at its root; a standalone skill bundle carries it under `scripts/engine/`. No benchmark story or private service is required. Users give natural language; technical formats and these implementation requirements belong in the skill/adapter.

## Initial story

1. Preserve original work/segment/shot text, IDs, numbering, order and explicit times. No shot crosses its segment. Allocate unspecified shot durations within the fixed segment; camera cuts are not new business shots. Clear explicit timed blocks may be copied deterministically without a model routing call; ordinary wishes/questions use one conversational intake. A clear story with omitted duration proceeds with concise model-chosen timing >0 and <=300 seconds; missing duration alone never warrants a question. Explicit times remain authoritative and chosen timing must not be presented as user-specified. Do not ask follow-up questions. Choose a conservative interpretation for harmless ambiguity and include doubts/choices with the output. Impossible mandatory timing or unusable input is an explicit error; never silently trim a mandatory over-300-second story.
2. Inspect every reference, bind its index to real cast/prop/location identities, and record concrete geometric choices in `reference_usage`; disclose uncertain identity. Preserve recognizable silhouette, color cues, layout, openings and required landmarks, not textures or detailed reconstruction. A segment is not a location.
3. Establish one shared identity/location/prop registry and coarse state timeline for the whole story. Before choosing cameras, make plot-critical states, ownership/support, before/after positions, safe-region arrivals and timed reveals executable. A rescue must reach its region, a placed object must have a support, and a carried object must travel with its owner. Returning to a scene preserves prior state. Same person in another costume keeps identity.
4. Author one compact complete `buildScene` module using the helpers and absolute tracks. Plan each camera inside this authoring call. No extra planning call, imagined tool result or video export. Detailed performance is optional; coarse outcomes and readable framing are not. A few discrete poses/keys and simple objects suffice.
5. Actually compile and replay the full timeline. Verify all original shots, segment/total times, boundaries, A→B→A seek consistency, finite state and dependencies. First resolve known render/structural failures (including missing camera coverage) without a redundant whole-story model review. Once technically usable, visually review paginated real start/middle/end evidence plus source/track facts and references. Module/JSON validity alone is not rendering or semantic success.
6. Repair demonstrated factual/technical blockers collectively with local edits: **initial candidate + at most two corrections**. Partial/invalid candidates use the same allowance. Confirmed output-token exhaustion before any renderable candidate permits at most one compact complete-module recovery, within that same correction allowance. Retain the original task/references and every required shot; never continue truncated code or fabricate a generic fallback. Use low effort for this technical recovery; an increased output cap requires a verified provider configuration. If a renderable candidate already exists, keep it instead of restarting. After the first correction, stop if only previously attempted semantic findings remain and no new defect appeared; retain them as unresolved, never as a pass. Reserve the second correction for render/structure recovery or a genuinely new/regressed blocker, using a simpler different approach rather than repeating the same fix. Second-correction edit targets exclude previously attempted semantic issues even when a new issue permits this call; old findings stay recorded, not silently passed. Never replan or beautify the whole story. A 600-second engineering target is soft and never grants extra attempts. Keep the last renderable candidate; retain unresolved findings at the limit. Evidence uncertainty remains an unresolved quality result and never by itself spends a source correction. Never fabricate an empty generic story or claim user approval.
7. Package source, serialized project, original shot plan, reference bindings, actual review evidence, timing, model settings, hashes and limitations. Video is a separate host step. Report scene usability, structure and semantic quality separately. Original/actual-sent media and raw model responses stay auditable.

## Product boundary and acceptance

Deliver a renderable, editable layout and camera plan for subsequent video generation, not finished character animation. Technical render/timing checks, coarse story/path/camera quality, and allowed proxy details are separate results. Static holding, standing, sitting, embracing and support contacts may intersect or have approximate gaps while ownership and the coarse situation remain readable. Do not spend a correction on these contact details, exact feet/floor alignment, hand grip, gait, combat choreography or precise limb angles. A small grazing overlap during travel is also acceptable; a body travelling straight through a solid wall/door/rail/furniture is not. Do not treat an automatic collision/contact warning as confirmed story failure without its actual context.

The quality priorities are clear travel routes and destinations, requested POV/framing and visibility at decisive times, stable carried-object relationships, sensible actor facing, and essential before/after story states. Report a remaining problem separately and still package the last renderable scene. A local quality issue never authorizes unbounded retries or withholding an otherwise renderable output. Seko retains every supplied segment/shot and its timing; ordinary stories are judged by their requested events and camera/space relationships, without inventing shot-level requirements.

## Minimal readable geometry

Every human, including extras, uses **the same seven primitives** via `helpers.person`: sphere head, box nose pointing local +Z, cylinder torso, two cylinder arms and two cylinder legs. No large single-cylinder substitute, fingers, hand/foot meshes or decorative anatomy. This fixed cheap silhouette replaces detail selection; do not build custom rigs. Stable color, height and name distinguish identities.

Limbs have fixed length and rotate only at shoulder/hip; never scale them or translate their endpoints. Default to relaxed arms beside the body and a normal stand/sit. Unsupported optional crouch/kneel/combat/gait may stay standing or seated; do not lower a standing body through floors/furniture to imitate them. If a nonessential gesture looks broken, remove that gesture. Brief reach/contact returns to relaxed arms afterwards unless holding/supporting still requires it. Face the interlocutor or target in dialogue/interaction and the travel direction during movement; the nose makes direction visible. Do not rotate a close-up character solely toward the camera if that contradicts the story.

Plan clear movement corridors before dressing the environment. Doorways are real gaps between wall pieces, not painted rectangles on a solid wall. Open/move the door leaf before passage; route via the opening and around furniture/railings with a few timed waypoints. Preserve required barriers and geography. For nonessential clutter, simplify or relocate the obstacle instead of walking through it. Prefer helpers.actorTimeline for a small number of travel/hold clips; its interval boundaries prevent unintended interpolation across scene resets. Cross-scene resets use step changes at actual cuts; travel within a scene must not teleport or interpolate through obstacles. A clear body route through a solid barrier is a factual defect; edge grazing, stationary pose/contact intersections and a co-moving held prop are not walking-through defects. Inspect moving endpoints and motion-audit event frames.

Carried props follow the carrier/hand with a stable local offset. Prefer helpers.attachProp with an explicit local offset; do not preserve a distant table-world position as the hand offset. Coarse hand/prop penetration is acceptable and cheaper than precise contact. People moving furniture share its path progress or a stable group-local offset; independent trajectories must not sweep through one another. At handoff/release, derive the next variant from the current world transform rather than its construction position. Their held/released variants share one event transform, position and orientation; they never drift independently while being carried. Keep critical handoff/arrival/reveal results visible at actual event times, not just the first frame. Use existing focus/event checkpoints at these times, within the current evidence budget. Required visible relationships need positive image/track evidence; absence of a contradiction outside the frame is not proof. A pose may be coarse; ownership and destinations remain readable.

Aim for <=3 visible primitive components per logical prop; people are exempt. Cylinders/spheres and boxes are enough. A few functional extras are allowed for required wheels, openings, supports or contact points; explain the exception briefly. Group/anchor nodes do not count. Do not evade the budget by renaming one decorated object into many subobjects. Environments use sparse boundaries plus required entrances, paths, furniture and landmarks; an entire location is not one prop. Avoid detailed merchandise, linked chains, many rubble pieces and large opaque fog/glow shells. A thin marked cylinder can convey a chain, a few blocks a rockfall. Preserve important spatial relationships and readable load-bearing contact.

Optional: gait, repeated mining/combat gestures, expressions, fingers, smooth physical dynamics and detailed surfaces. Required: necessary actor/prop presence, plot-critical state, displacement and destination, readable ownership/support, narrative reveal and important height/spatial relationships. A pose is essential only when it changes these outcomes (for example seated versus standing at a table, or supporting a load). Small incidental arm-angle differences, greeting/nodding gestures and fine performance are optional, even if mentioned in the story. Record these simplifications without a source correction. Standing/travel facing must remain sensible, and nonessential reaching must not leave an arm extended indefinitely. A brief approach/contact and changed endpoints can convey an essential interaction; do not build detailed action choreography. Keep original dialogue/omitted performance as metadata; do not add subtitles, audio, lip-sync or beauty passes. On nonessential artistic ambiguity choose a lightweight interpretation; disclose the conservative interpretation and remaining ambiguity with the result; do not wait for user confirmation.

## Static-first camera planning

The business deliverable is an editable 3D storyboard. Original shot IDs/order/durations, narrative facts, framing scale, POV and reference identities remain authoritative. Decorative imported push/pan/orbit/shake may become a static equivalent preserving meaning. An explicit user lock on a particular path is binding. Do not turn inserts into generic wide shots merely to keep everything in frame.

For each shot, add compact `camera_plan` per the source contract: state what the audience must see (necessary actor/prop/contact/result), then choose one safe static composition first. Dialogue, reactions, crowded interiors and layered scenes often benefit from a stable view. Main subjects/props must be correctly framed at their **actual time-specific positions**; an initial world coordinate is not a future-state lookup. Preserve axis, deliberate close-ups/POV and purposeful pre-reveal concealment. Once revealed, the necessary subject must read clearly. Include the necessary visible subjects in `camera_plan.subjects`, not irrelevant cropped actors.

Move only when it materially helps follow a subject between important places/objects, reveal information at the right time, explain a spatial relationship, or satisfy a user-locked path. Vague cinematic/emotional variety is insufficient. In complex occlusion-prone geometry prefer a clear fixed view unless movement conveys necessary information. If movement is needed, use a short simple path and check start/middle/end and the decisive event. No moving-shot quota.

A fixed shot has constant position, orientation/target, FOV and roll for its entire interval; moving a target is still motion. Fit required displacement and endpoints, or choose follow if a stable view would clip them. Usually one camera interval per business shot; never merge/drop shots. Keep cameras outside solid geometry and required viewing rays clear. Preserve an explicit POV/follow/over-shoulder relationship when clearing a viewing ray; do not replace it with a generic distant overhead view. Prefer a safer viewpoint or local cutaway of an **optional** occluder. Do not erase necessary objects/environment, make important objects transparent, shrink away a contact, or request decorative geometry to solve framing.

Review coarse outcomes against actual images/tracks, not only the authored camera plan. Fine acting and decorative motion can be omitted. Hidden necessary subjects, wrong POV or direction, visibly detached props, wrong plot-critical state and missing causal results still need a correction. Ignore contact gaps within the supplied coarse tolerance and minor intersections that do not obscure ownership or the story. A screenshot is a readable proxy, not a precision physics test. Structured review distinguishes `core` from optional simplification; the host derives severity. A review-format failure cannot trigger a source repair.

## Follow-up

Use the latest delivered project/source and saved user turns; do not restart from zero. Modify only the requested shots/entities plus directly dependent attachments/cameras/boundaries and unresolved factual issues. Preserve all unrelated identities, geometry, timing and accepted camera decisions. **One edit candidate + at most one correction.** Retain the prior immutable revision and checks for unchanged content. Reject stale revision overwrites. Source refinement refuses manual overlays; use supported workspace edits for those.

Source corrections use numbered current-source lines and its exact SHA-256. Line ranges address the original source snapshot atomically, never shifted lines or guessed shot IDs. Do not repeat old source substrings. Failed or overlapping patches retain the original source and unresolved feedback, with concrete mismatch diagnostics; no fuzzy replacement or silent whole-story rewrite. After an edit, replay technical checks globally and visually recheck every changed image/metadata/affected track interval, unresolved check and prior warning shot. Proven old/new hidden intervals may limit visibility-dependent scope; unknown dependencies remain conservative. Changed timing/shot topology or reference identity requires full review. Do not discard evidence to save time or introduce an extra model call just to polish.

## Evidence and host boundaries

Reference images and tool images must remain present in the appropriate model call and auditable by hash. Host media compression and deterministic syntax/input adapters never consume reflection/correction budget. Keep original text and references immutable. Context management preserves the original request, latest authoritative source and necessary evidence; bounded recent user turns survive, while earlier redundant history may be archived without speculative compression calls. A network/provider/context-limit failure is not a geometry defect; preserve the latest valid project and incomplete-review evidence without spending a correction on the network.

Focus checks are sampled point/ray observations, not whole-object visibility or semantic proof. Composite geometry may be visible around an occluded empty center, and positive translucent alpha may be visible below 0.5; hidden/zero-alpha or genuinely occluded geometry remains invalid. Always combine tool facts with actual screenshots. Local replay is mandatory; raw model output, actual model media, warnings and unresolved findings remain available even when the business webpage does not display internal self-check results.

Actual geometry evidence includes finite opaque AABB separation between declared subjects, resolving a head/hand to its registered character body. These distances are advisory, not a centimetre-level acceptance threshold. Static overlap or a contact gap alone cannot create a source defect. Check visible ownership, relative drift over time, key support state and readable framing; zero gap alone does not prove these. Executable parent bindings/tracks and visible ownership may supply evidence even when a separate pair sample is absent. Keep the original subject/contact/position requirement intact when summarizing a shot. This is deterministic evidence, not an extra model call or correction.

Root-motion facts check all influencing transform keys over the full shot. A root proven stationary cannot supply a required entry/exit/arrival by movement; a static endpoint-looking pose is not evidence that the action occurred. Consider actual visible identity/pose variants and child motion separately. This deterministic fact adds no model call.
