# Review actual coarse story outcomes

Read ORIGINAL_TASK, actual source/track/subject facts, technical checks and every supplied screenshot/reference sheet. Inspect exactly CURRENT_REVIEW_SCOPE. A generated camera_plan describes intent, never proof of the original user's request or of the rendered result. Finite samples do not certify every frame.

For each shot, compare its necessary coarse result with the **actual** image and tracks. Return `core`:
- `met`: required subject, framing/POV, coarse pose, position/ownership, contact, displacement/arrival and reveal order are supported by the evidence wherever the original shot requires them.
- `missing`: one of those required results is absent, contradicted or hidden. Give a concrete scoped issue and minimal correction.
- `uncertain`: necessary evidence cannot establish a required result. State the specific uncertainty. Do not claim success from intended metadata.

The host derives pass/warning/blocker from this assessment. A `missing` or `uncertain` core cannot be downgraded to a warning or quality pass. Only a demonstrated scene defect justifies a source correction. `uncertain` means retain an unresolved quality finding; it is not permission to change geometry or to spend a correction merely collecting more evidence. `simplification` only describes the following optional detail, **after** the core result is met:
- `none`: no relevant deviation.
- `appearance`: texture, clothing decoration, fingers, tiny surface detail, intricate chain links or photorealism.
- `fine_acting`: expressive faces, smooth gait, repeated gestures, fine muscle/arm performance or realistic physics, while the coarse pose, contact and outcome remain correct.
- `decorative_camera`: optional smoothness, ornamental push/pan/shake, while required framing/POV, narrative reveal and readable subject remain correct under the static-first policy.

A standing actor instead of the required collapsed/seated one, a stone floating above the actor instead of resting on them, a carried/placed prop detached from its owner/support, failure to leave/enter a named region, or an establishing view replacing a required readable prop insert is **not** optional fine acting or camera polish. Simple discrete poses and a few position keys suffice; do not demand realistic dynamics. A cropped person in a deliberate prop insert or POV is allowed. Do not require every actor's head in every shot. Dialogue/emotions/fine gestures can remain metadata. Define the core by the shot's plot and spatial outcome, not by turning every incidental acting verb into an animation requirement. Do not reopen an otherwise correct shot just to retime a fine gesture. A close-up may crop an identity or support cue already established in continuous prior shots; do not demand all contextual props visible again unless the original framing specifically requires them. No subtitles/audio or decorative upgrade is required.

Actual geometry relations provide opaque AABB gaps for declared subjects (head/hand anchors resolve to their registered character body). A positive gap means those opaque surfaces are spatially separated even when one is above another in a 2D frame. If the original shot requires resting/supporting/holding contact between them, this contradicts that coarse outcome: mark core missing and propose a local position/pose correction. Zero gap does not prove contact; translucent or unavailable geometry is unknown. Do not substitute "kneeling underneath" for "back supports the rock" or discard the original contact requirement from your requirement summary.

Root-motion evidence checks the full shot interval and all interior transform keys, not just three screenshots. `world_root_transform_constant_over_shot:true` means that declared body root has no translation/turn/scale change through the shot. A still pose near a boundary does not prove an exit/entry/climb/arrival happened. When the original requires displacement, compare the actual old/new positions and visible actor variants; if no transition exists, core is missing even if a frozen pose resembles its intended endpoint. Do not infer a completed action from a single pose. This root fact does not forbid child limb acting or intentional static shots.

Use track facts and start/middle/end samples to distinguish an actual move from repeated stills, and intentional pre-reveal concealment from a missing post-reveal subject. Judge a shot's required event at its relevant time. A glimpse behind an unrelated foreground object is insufficient if it hides the necessary result. Returning scenes retain ownership and state. Do not invent cast, events or requirements.

Focus checks are finite point/ray tests, not full silhouettes or semantic acceptance. A visible composite may surround an occluded empty center; translucent target geometry may be visible below opacity 0.5. Zero-opacity/hidden geometry cannot support visibility. True opaque obstruction/offscreen content remains a blocker when it defeats the intended subject. Read actual frames before interpreting a failed checkpoint.

Report all demonstrated core omissions together in the first review, so the bounded correction can address them locally. The issues list is for observed scene contradictions with an actionable local source fix. Missing pair/time samples alone belong in an uncertain shot observation, not a speculative source-fix request. Do not ask a source editor to invent audit receipts or certify unsampled frames. Prefer a safe fixed view or cutaway of an optional occluder; preserve required objects, geography, motion and contact. Do not spend corrections on costumes, gait, expressions or cinematic polish. Do not erase the environment, remove required subjects or make important opaque objects transparent to pass checks.

## Required contact audit

Every shot row includes `contacts`. List every pair whose touching/holding/support is necessary to the original shot and claimed in your observed outcome. Use only pair IDs and exact times from that shot's actual geometry_relations/times table, at the portion where the original requires contact. For holding/placing, cite the held portion, not only the final table landing. For a prop detached from a still hand, or a nearby actor without touching, report missing contact, not fine_acting. If original contact is required but the needed pair/time is absent from evidence, say core uncertain and explain the gap; do not invent numbers, claim success, or request geometry changes solely to create an audit table. Required contact means plot-relevant ownership/support or the primary interaction, not every incidental limb gesture. Use [] when no contact is required. Do NOT make unrelated actors/objects touch merely because their gap is positive. A simple body may stand apart while talking, reacting or watching. The host cross-checks your required pairs: positive opaque AABB separation at a claimed contact time overrides a met verdict. Zero separation alone never proves contact. Contact gaps already appear in ACTUAL_FACTS; this explicit audit prevents ignoring them when summarizing an otherwise readable scene.
