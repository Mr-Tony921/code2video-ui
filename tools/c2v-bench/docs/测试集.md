可测试。

```text
Make a 45-second 16:9 narrated explainer for curious twelve-year-olds, playing a classroom screen, drawn by hand in chalk on a blackboard. Use only the facts and sources below; don't add claims or numbers, and say "about" for anything rounded.

Write about 80 spoken words on five boards: a question or a surprising fact, the mechanism in two steps, one worked case, and the takeaway that answers the opening. Each board holds one idea and one main drawing; everything on it, lettering included, is drawn on stroke by stroke at a hand's speed, each mark starting on the word that names it, by a drawn hand holding the tool. If the voice is synthetic, write "Narration: synthetic voice" small on that board, after everything else.

Topic: "Why the Moon changes shape"
Facts and sources: NASA Science, "Moon Phases"
Language: English
Closing line and web address, if any: "Look up tonight." · example.org/moon

风格：
二维黑板手绘风格。画面以深绿色木框黑板为底，带有粉笔擦拭的磨砂斑驳纹理。图形与文字均采用粗糙颗粒感的白色、黄色与浅蓝色粉笔线条勾勒及排线填充，造型简化且高度平面图解化。色彩对比鲜明，无复杂光影，配合扁平剪贴风格的绘制手部，呈现质朴清晰的板书演示质感。
```

- 每板一个概念与主图，关键词 1–4 词；约停一秒后擦除；有书写声、无配乐
